Privacy & service terms

How Wavix Internet handles installation enquiries, payments and project details.

Wavix Internet provides internet installation, CCTV installation, CAT6 cabling, router sales and network support for homes, apartments, estates, businesses and commercial buildings in Kenya.

Quote Information

  • We may ask for your name, phone number, location, building type, camera count, router needs and cabling distance.
  • These details are used to prepare a site quote and recommend suitable equipment.
  • WhatsApp messages are handled through WhatsApp on your device.

Project Photos

  • Completed work photos may be shown on the website only when approved by the project owner.
  • Private areas, passwords, router logins and sensitive camera views should not be published.
  • Customers can request removal of identifiable project photos.

Payments

  • Stablecoin prices may be quoted in USDT, USDC or RLUSD.
  • Confirm the final amount, wallet address and network before sending any crypto payment.
  • Hardware stock and installation prices can change after site survey.

Installation Terms

  • Final project cost depends on building size, cable route, device count, mounting height and travel.
  • Used Tenda and TP-Link routers are sold as tested used equipment unless stated otherwise.
  • Customers should keep router passwords, CCTV passwords and admin credentials private after handover.
